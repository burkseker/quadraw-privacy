Privacy Policy for Quadraw

Effective date: 20 September 2026

Quadraw ("the game") is a turn-based territory game for Android, published by Burak Seker ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the game does with data. It is short because the game does very little: it is played entirely on your device, has no accounts, and asks you for nothing.

What we collect

Nothing. We operate no servers and receive no data from the game. We cannot see who plays it, what settings you choose, or how you do.

What stays on your device

The game saves the following on the device itself, in its own private storage, and never sends it anywhere:

  • your settings — language, board palette, your seat colour, accessibility marks, sound and haptics;
  • your record — wins, streaks, largest territory and achievements;
  • your last dozen matches, kept so they can be replayed, and one match in progress so it can be resumed;
  • whether you have been shown the tutorial.

Deleting this is entirely in your hands: Settings → Data → Reset record and match history clears it, and uninstalling the game removes all of it.

Advertising

The game shows one full-screen advertisement when a match ends, at most once every third finished match. The advertisements are served by Google AdMob, which is part of Google. To serve them, the AdMob software inside the game collects and processes data about the device — including your device's advertising identifier, IP address, approximate location derived from that address, device and operating system details, and your interactions with the advertisements.

This processing is Google's, not ours; we receive only aggregate counts of how many advertisements were shown. How Google uses this data is described in:

  • Google's Privacy Policy — https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services — https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

You can reset or delete your advertising identifier at any time in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads, which stops advertisements being tied to the previous identifier.

Consent, if you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland

Before the game requests a single advertisement, it shows you a consent form served through Google's User Messaging Platform, a Google-certified consent management platform. Nothing is requested until you have answered it, and your answer decides whether the advertisements you see are personalised.

You can change that answer whenever you like: the form is reachable again from Settings → Privacy. That row appears only where the form was shown in the first place, because consent that cannot be withdrawn as easily as it was given is not consent.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect anything from them. It also collects nothing from anybody, which is the more useful half of that sentence.

Permissions

The game requests internet access, network state and the advertising identifier. All of them come from the advertising software and exist only so an advertisement can be fetched and counted. The game asks for no camera, no microphone, no location, no contacts, no files and no storage.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address and the effective date above will change with it. Material changes will also be noted in the game's release notes.
